“Prefix exact” compares every continuous ledger entry through the first
segment request count against the saved first-run ledger. The first resumed
evaluation has identical coordinates in all five cases. The Cu31 star marks
the only metal result whose runner stopped before writing a final summary;
its request total is reconstructed from the two saved ledgers. Subsequent
fresh-calculator arithmetic differs by case. Those are observations of force,
energy, and coordinate values at repeated calculations on the same geometry;
they do not establish a particular internal cause. In particular, GFN2-xTB
SCF state is a possible mechanism for the molecular differences, but the
artifacts do not prove that mechanism.

The `fresh.json` files qualify every listed landing against the recorded fixed
cell, energy error, and `fmax <= 0.01 eV/Å` check used by the runner. This is a
checkpoint and numerical replay qualification, not a claim of search quality,
chemical stability, or first-principles validity. The Cu inputs came from the
verified-ritz seed-11 artifacts; bicyclobutane and both butadiene arms use the
source/provenance recorded in each output directory. EMT was used for the Cu
cases and GFN2-xTB accuracy `.001` for the molecular cases. No electronic
restart state was serialized.

The corrected offline audit derives request costs from the ledgers and result
objects, and derives final coordinates and acceptance sequences from the two
result JSON files; it does not depend on an optional runner summary. All five
cases pass initial-plus-record request accounting and have nonempty fresh
qualification sets matching the recorded minima counts.
