# CuO64 frozen biased-quench history diagnosis

2026-09-11. This is a fixed-objective local optimizer diagnosis using the
already recorded CuO64 failed biased-quench points. It is not a whole-SSW
search or evidence that a larger history improves discovery.

The four independent fresh quenches used the same frozen inputs, model and
search settings, changing only Safe-total history capacity from 10 to 500.
History 10 reached the 300-step maximum in both seeds: seed 7 used 312
optimizer requests and seed 101 used 318. History 500 converged in 161 and
193 steps, using 166 and 195 optimizer requests respectively. Each case has
one final fresh endpoint request. The two fresh checks of the original failed
points are separate charged requests. The complete accounting is therefore
991 optimizer + 4 final-fresh + 2 failed-point checks = **997 E/F requests**.

| seed | history | optimizer status | steps | optimizer E/F | final fresh E (eV) | final fresh projected-gradient norm | total |
|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 7 | 10 | maxiter | 300 | 312 | -305.4070516141 | 0.19056446 | 313 |
| 101 | 10 | maxiter | 300 | 318 | -305.3454302068 | 0.02022232 | 319 |
| 7 | 500 | converged | 161 | 166 | -305.5567019644 | 0.00088759 | 167 |
| 101 | 500 | converged | 193 | 195 | -305.3455113915 | 0.00068032 | 196 |

The fresh endpoint is a new evaluation of each optimizer output. The final
fresh values show lower physical energy and smaller projected gradient for the
history-500 runs, but this remains a local fixed-point diagnosis. The
history-10 trajectories are not claimed to be bitwise replays of the earlier
baseline attempts: the recorded baseline attempted requests were 315 and 313,
and the fresh diagnosis reconstructs the failed objective independently.

The two failed-point checks reproduce the source biased-gradient norm at the
same frozen points. For seed 7 the matching source evaluation is request 891
and the norm difference is `2.1094e-15`; for seed 101 it is request 1136 and
the difference is `1.6168e-15`. This verifies the failure-point objective
before comparing history capacity. Full machine-readable accounting is in
`evidence/cuo64-frozen-quench-history/diagnosis/summary.json`, generated by
`summarize_frozen_quench_history.py`.

The separately prepared two-seed history-500 whole-SSW control has no result
in this record and is excluded. No claim about whole-search landing rate,
basin discovery, or general optimizer superiority follows from this diagnosis.
