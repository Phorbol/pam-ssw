## Height and history audit

For every prepared stage, the saved `height_preparation` gives an angle of 87°
within `5.7e-14°` and a criterion residual within `6.2e-15` in the declared
scaled joint metric.  Seed 101 had 13 prepared stages and seed 7 had 12.  The
history lengths are exactly `0..12` and `0..11`; the returned term lengths are
exactly one larger, showing that each new Gaussian was appended to the prior
history.  No stage reported `nonpositive_height`.

The saved `height_input.background_force` is the negative of the measured
joint gradient used by the policy.  The single measured displaced background
call is counted under `height_requests=1` per prepared stage and is reused for
height preparation; no additional height evaluation is charged.  The saved
history is passed into the policy and the resulting frozen terms are retained
through the subsequent biased quench.  This verifies the intended physical +
LS + old-Gaussian objective accounting for this run; it does not prove native
VC equivalence.

The final failed stages were censored biased-quench/evaluation paths after the
prepared stages.  These are preserved in the per-arm `result.json` and
`evaluations.jsonl`; the report does not reinterpret them as early quench or
nonpositive-height exits.

## Limits

The run has four requested outer attempts, one input, two seeds and a bounded
MACE finite-cell backend.  It shows that the analytic angle condition and
history/background-force wiring were exercised, while neither this condition
nor the zero valid proposals supports an efficiency, basin, phase or chemical
stability claim.  The reproducible aggregate is
`research/ga_ssw/evidence/fe7c3-80-angle-comparison/summary.json`, generated by
the adjacent `summarize.py`.
