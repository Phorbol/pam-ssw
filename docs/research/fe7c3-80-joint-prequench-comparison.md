# Fe7C3 joint LS prequench comparison audit

The frozen joint-prequench results are under
`research/ga_ssw/evidence/fe7c3-80-joint-prequench-comparison/comparison/`.
The matched fixed-cell controls are under
`research/ga_ssw/evidence/fe7c3-80-ls-filter-comparison/comparison/`.
Both use the same Fe7C3 80-atom input (same SHA256), MACE model/hash,
strain length, LS tables, Gaussian/search settings, pressure, tolerances and
two requested outer steps per arm. The only planned kernel difference is
`ls_prequench='joint'` versus fixed-cell preparation.

The derived audit is
`comparison/joint-prequench-summary.json`, generated by
`research/ga_ssw/summarize_fe7c3_joint_prequench.py`. It covers all 8 requested
arm-step slots (four arms, two steps each). Every arm has an initial landing
plus no accepted proposal; each has one checked original fresh landing. All
joint records retain 456 frozen LS pairs.

Joint total charged cost is 5,777 EFS versus 5,502 for fixed-cell controls,
an aggregate difference of 275 EFS. The direct preparation counts are 15
versus 10 requests per outer step for `ls_all`, and
14 versus 9 for `ls_filter`; thus direct preparation accounts for 40 EFS in
aggregate, while the remaining 235 EFS is downstream search behavior. Per-arm joint
versus fixed costs are respectively 754 versus 667 (seed 7, all), 1,682
versus 839 (seed 101, all), 1,998 versus 1,998 (seed 7, filtered; both
censored), and 1,343 versus 1,998 (seed 101, filtered).

For every completed joint prequench, the softened joint gradient, softened
atomic force maximum, and softened stress maximum are serialized. The
physical fresh checks remain separate: they compare the recorded landing
energy/forces/stress after calculator replacement and report physical fmax and
stress gates. No softened atomic small-force value is presented as a physical
force certificate. The joint prequench also records physical ΔH/N response,
LS energy before/after, volume before/after, and explicit request counts.

This is a fixed-parameter Fe7C3 comparison. It does not establish a general
SSW improvement, default promotion, or a causal conclusion from the unequal
downstream costs. Censored arms remain censored in the denominator.

The complete primary failure count is 7 maxiter and1 budget exhaustion for
joint preparation, versus6 maxiter and2 budget exhaustions for fixed
preparation. All8 attempts in each group entered and paid for evaluations;
no requested attempt is removed. The4 landings per group in the summary are
only the4 initial states, not4 generated candidates. Charged costs reconcile
both raw ledgers and per-record counters, including4 independent fresh checks
per group.

Across all8 joint preparations the softened joint norm is
1.53979e-4–6.10398e-4, softened atomic fmax2.70465e-5–1.71333e-4,
and softened stress maximum1.10885e-6–6.08797e-6. These are softened-objective
quantities. Volumes increase by8.7333%–16.4710% and physical ΔH/N by
0.0554955–0.1301195 eV/atom. The preparation therefore changes the starting
state substantially even while passing its mathematical stationarity gate.
No soft-preparation Hessian or physical phase qualification is inferred.
