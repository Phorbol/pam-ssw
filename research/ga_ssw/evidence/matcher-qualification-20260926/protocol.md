# Geometry matcher qualification (zero PES)

**Question.** Do the existing ordered PAM pool matcher, ASE's `geometry.distance(..., permute=True)/sqrt(N)`, and pymatgen's `HungarianOrderMatcher` recognize known rigid-body and same-species relabelings of the same archived geometry at the existing 0.1 Å threshold? Does the assignment-based method remain numerically usable on a highly symmetric C60 geometry?

**Competing explanations.** (1) Missed pool identity matches arise from the exact atom-order constraint in the current matcher, and species-preserving assignment remedies that specific invariance gap. (2) The mature alternatives are themselves approximate or symmetry-degenerate, so passing transformed self-pairs does not qualify them as basin identity tests. This probe distinguishes matcher invariance on known same-geometry transformations; it does not adjudicate physical basin identity.

**Inputs and only comparisons.** Use all 30 archived C4H6 initial/first-topology representative geometries in the frozen topology audit, the ASE-built C60 geometry, and two archived C60 geometries. For every geometry, compare an exact copy and three deterministic seeds each of rigid transform, same-species permutation, and rigid-plus-permutation. Pairwise comparisons among the three archived/ASE C60 references are raw diagnostic values only; no pair receives a true-match label. Source SHA-256 values are checked/recorded. No PES, optimization, new thresholds, multistart search, factorial enumeration, or reward changes.

**Fixed methods and metric.** All methods use RMSD threshold 0.1 Å. The current `MinimaArchive` uses its existing ordered nonperiodic Kabsch matcher (`energy_tol=0.001`, equal comparison energies); ASE uses its current inertia-axis/greedy same-species permutation procedure divided by `sqrt(N)`; pymatgen uses `HungarianOrderMatcher(reference).match(query)` and its returned RMSD. Matcher-call wall times are recorded separately from input loading and transformation construction. Exceptions and threshold misses remain in the output. Report exact, rigid, permutation, and combined pass counts with query denominators and timing. The task is geometric identity qualification only, not a scientific search-efficiency comparison.

**Interpretation and stop rule.** If ordered matching fails same-species permutations while an assignment matcher passes them, the evidence supports a specific invariance limitation of atom-order matching. It does not show a historical pool was contaminated or that any two different structures are the same minimum. Stable atom indices in historical trajectories do not rule out real exchanges/reorientations returning to the same unlabeled geometry; conversely, this probe does not establish that such relabeling contaminated the historical pool. Stop after this fixed panel. Preserve symmetry-related failures, exceptions, and misses as qualification limits; do not tune methods or thresholds.

**Budget/provenance.** Zero PES evaluations; one CPU-MISC job, one task, at most 5 minutes, `sjtu-caoxiaoming` / `rush-cpu`. Output is a new JSON path and refuses overwrite. No login-node analysis.
