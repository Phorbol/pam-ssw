## Counterevidence and limits

Lower numerical cost is not lower discovered energy. AlOH seed71 reaches
ΔE=-1.70026 eV with ASE, -0.96797 eV with Safe-total and -0.86826 eV with SciPy.
AlOH seed83 instead favors Safe-total's lowest landing (-0.70716 eV versus
-0.44274/-0.43933 eV). TiO2 seed71 lowest landings are similar (~-0.82 eV);
seed83 has no downhill landing for any method. Three steps cannot rank global
optimization. Later paths differ after optimizer and MC outcomes diverge.

Native termination criteria differ: SciPy/ASE often overconverge relative to the
common endpoint certificate. Prior frozen-task first-common-certificate costs
provide separate evidence, but this end-to-end comparison does not isolate
stopping-rule cost from step/line-search quality. StructureMatcher groups are
approximate geometric groups, not phase labels or independently proven basins.
The certificate is numerical on MACE OMAT; chemistry and DFT phase validation
are not established by it. This is joint log-strain VC, not the 2014 alternating
block schedule.

## Decision

Retain Safe-total as the supported candidate for joint-VC development. Do not
change generic defaults, claim universal superiority, retune on these four
pairs, or expand to production-scale trajectories. The specific concern that
its favorable local behavior disappears immediately in complete joint-VC steps
is not supported by this panel. Next method comparisons should target a new
scientific question; the immediate parallel priority is a valid pool identity
contract before interpreting LS plus PAM selection as structural exploration.
