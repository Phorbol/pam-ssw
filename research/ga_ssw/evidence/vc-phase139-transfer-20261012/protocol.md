# Phase-139 TiO₂ initial-geometry transfer for the block-VC atomic contribution test

## Question

Does the phase-87 target-arrival behavior of the existing 48-atom block-VC search persist from a second paper-derived 48-atom TiO₂ structure, or is it sensitive to the starting geometry? The paired comparison remains the one already frozen in `vc-atomic-contribution-20261012`: `cell_only` (`atomic_period=21`, no atomic climb in 20 outer steps) versus `cell_plus_atomic` (`atomic_period=2`). This phase-139 transfer changes only the initial structure and uses the new paired seeds `26101251/52`; all SSW, block, temperature, direction, finite rotation-release, quench, target, model, precision, request and wall settings are inherited unchanged. The phase-139 comparison is within-seed paired. Its descriptive comparison with phase-87 outcomes is not seed-matched, so a between-start difference suggests start sensitivity but cannot isolate initial geometry from random-seed variation.

## Interpretation and stopping

If the phase-87 panel reaches anatase while neither phase-139 seed does, that is evidence consistent with starting-geometry sensitivity under the tested seeds and budgets; it does not prove a geometry-only causal effect because phase-87 used different seeds. If phase-139 reaches anatase in both arms, the target capability is not unique to phase-87, but this small transfer does not establish robust generality. Mixed outcomes remain inconclusive. The atomic contribution claim is limited to the within-input paired outcomes and costs; do not conflate the phase transfer with a treatment comparison.

No parameter tuning, retry, continuation, new seed, or extended search is authorized by this protocol. A paid calculator/nonfinite failure stops further paid work and later arms, preserving the failure and last accepted state. Request/wall limits remain censored outcomes, and later zero-request rows are marked not started for lack of budget. The runner does not submit jobs.
