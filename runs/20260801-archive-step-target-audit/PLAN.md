# U-T0 archive-scaled uphill-target audit

Reconstruct the exact macro-action target history from the twelve completed
S-CR1 accepted-minimum logs. This audit performs no PES evaluation and changes
no search configuration. It only decides whether the archive-scale block is
active enough to justify one fixed-0.8-eV causal gate.

U-T1 is admitted when reconstruction and hashes close and at least two systems
spend more than 75% of completed actions away from the configured reference.
This is an activity gate, not evidence that fixed targeting is better.
